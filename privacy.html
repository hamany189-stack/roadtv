Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

RoadTV ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data, local storage, and service integration.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

RoadTV does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal information on external servers. We do not use third-party tracking, analytics, or behavioral advertising SDKs.

2. User Content & Local Data

  • Playlists & Accounts: Playlist URLs (such as M3U files) and user credentials you provide are stored strictly locally on your device.
  • OpenSubtitles API Key: Your personal API key is saved securely in your device's Keychain and is used solely to fetch subtitles requested by you.
  • Media Streaming: RoadTV acts purely as a player. Streaming requests occur directly between your device and the media servers specified in your own playlists.

3. iCloud Synchronization (Optional)

If you enable iCloud Sync via RoadTV Pro, your playlist URLs and favorite channel identifiers are synchronized securely across your Apple devices using Apple's private CloudKit database. We have no access to your iCloud data or Apple ID credentials.

4. In-App Purchases

All subscription and lifetime purchases are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit. We do not process, receive, or store any financial details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.
