RoadTV Support & Documentation
Important Notice: RoadTV is a media player application. It does not provide, host, or distribute any media content, playlists, or streaming channels. Users are responsible for providing their own legal content sources.

How do I add an M3U playlist?

Open RoadTV on your iPhone, navigate to Settings > Manage Sources, and tap the + button in the top corner. Enter your playlist URL or import a local file, then assign a name to it.

How does CarPlay integration work?

When connected to Apple CarPlay, RoadTV lets you browse your imported channel groups and manage media playback directly through CarPlay's interface, adhering to safe automotive driving standards.

What is RoadTV Pro?

RoadTV Pro unlocks advanced playback capabilities, iCloud playlist synchronization across Apple devices, and unlimited media casting options. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled anytime via your Apple ID Settings.

How do I restore my purchases?

If you reinstalled the app or set up a new device, open Settings > RoadTV Pro, scroll to the bottom, and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription will be verified via Apple StoreKit automatically.
